SHOPIFY LIVE READ-ONLY SETUP

This ZIP was built by modifying the exact project you uploaded.

Open:
backend/.env

Paste your own values into:
SHOPIFY_CLIENT_ID=
SHOPIFY_CLIENT_SECRET=

These are already prepared:
SHOPIFY_MODE=live
SHOPIFY_SHOP=your-development-store
SHOPIFY_API_VERSION=2026-07

Do NOT send the Client Secret in chat.
Do NOT commit backend/.env to Git.

Restart FastAPI, then from backend run:

python scripts\check_shopify.py

Expected:
Shop: your-development-store.myshopify.com
Authenticating with Shopify...
Authentication: OK
Orders returned: <number>

GET /api/v1/shopify/status
